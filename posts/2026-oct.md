## Methodology

As a brief summary of what i did here. For my gallery i collect and collate every art post on Tom [patreon](https://www.patreon.com/c/twokinds/posts) including the description. Whenever Tom post suggested sketches or finished artwork he adds some attribution in the form of `suggested by <someone>` which is something i can extract. 

Once the suggester information was extracted from the rest is relatively easy to present it in much the same way I used for the e6 tags. But for legibility i first got the top 25 suggesters with the most pieces credited to them and sliced the data accordingly and then i pivot the dataframe into a heatmap again.

But before showing the results some small notes on other changes
1- Improved the way i extract suggesters names some people where credited with multiple aliases but managed to coalesce them into a single name. This affected the results.
2- Added vanitas to the list of characters

### Limitations
There are a couple issues i should mention, first not all post have a suggester attached to it, sometime Tom forgets and piece is simply something he came up for/by himself. Second it is arbitrary text parsing so i did not get all of them. Sometimes usernames were weird, sometimes Tom did not added them proper or used a weird format etcetera. In particular I was not able to parse multiple suggesters so that information is excluded from the graphs. Alas i do not think this changes overall results much.

You can check [this csv](../results/graphs/tom_fischbach/october/23/oct23_suggesters.csv) to see the list of all suggestions and the user credited.

### Notable notes

Sinc the graphs are aggregated every quarter and this information was gathered on the 24th of october there may be some small artifacting on the rightmost edge of the graphs since Q4 just started on october 1st. I could fix that but i really do not mind much and really wanted to capture the comic anniversary. That beign said you should keep that in mind

## Discussion

So this is the second year of all tom picks, requiescat in pace [tkpolls](tkpolls.com) and I think it is interesting to see how the sketches have evolved over time. 

Overall i do not see that much change from last year tendencies remained. The main 4 and Kat still have a solid amount of sketches so far and of course the lynxverse is as healthy as ever. Likewise we still experience complete Laura collapse.

In other notes we also had a small decrease on the amount of Raine art, something i definetely felt, and surprised me since i thought Tom liked Raine a lot. But that was not the biggest loser here we had very sketches featuring Zen, Nibs, Adelaide, Willow and most suprising: Rosely, i thought she was well loved. 

Likewise as i implied only Kat remains somewhat sketched of all members of the modern trio 

And on the last note i added Vanitas this year, and honestly we did not get as much of this vixen art. Seems he popularity spiked on the last winter and then interest in her cooled

On to the suggesters. The biggest change i found was after correcting for alternative spellings (unsteady or unsteadyphoenix) unsteaddy became the person with the most attributed sketches of all time apparently. 

But other than that the overall trends remained constant. Well not quite, a very intersting thing i see is that i see no trend. If you check the last graph from 2025 and newer only specially for this year i do not see more than noise. All sugestion seem rather well spread which is weird.
